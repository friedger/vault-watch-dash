## What is Digital Independence Day?

- A recurring day of action on the **first Sunday of every month**
- One idea: **switch one service** from Big Tech to an open alternative
- Bring a friend, a family member or a neighbour along
- Small steps, repeated monthly, add up to real independence

---

## Why does it matter?

- A few companies control most of our **communication, data and attention**
- Their business model is **surveillance and lock-in**
- Their terms, prices and politics can change overnight
- Europe depends on infrastructure it does not control

> If you can't leave, you don't own it.

---

## Digital independence is a commons question

- Open protocols and open source are **digital commons**
- They belong to everyone and are cared for by communities
- Every switch strengthens the commons, not a shareholder
- Commons need **users, contributors and caretakers**: that's us

## Start small

1. **Pick one service** that bothers you most
2. **Install** the alternative today
3. **Move** your contacts or data over
4. **Tell** three people where to find you now
5. Come back next month and **switch the next one**

---

## Common worries

- *"Nobody I know uses it."*: be the first; network effects work both ways
- *"It's too complicated."*: that's why we do it together, today
- *"I have nothing to hide."*: it's about power and choice, not secrets
- *"It costs money."*: paying with money beats paying with your data
